Frequently Asked Questions

What is the duration of the internship? +
The internship duration varies based on the specific track chosen, typically ranging from 3 to 6 months to ensure comprehensive learning and project completion.
Is this internship completely online? +
Yes, currently our internship programs are fully remote, allowing you to participate from anywhere while maintaining flexible working hours.
Will I get a certificate after completion? +
Absolutely. Upon successful completion of the internship and assigned projects, you will receive an industry-recognized certificate of completion.
Are there any eligibility criteria? +
Basic knowledge of programming concepts is recommended. The program is ideal for college students, fresh graduates, and career changers.
Is there any cost involved? +
Please refer to our application page for specific details regarding stipends or any associated training costs depending on the program tier.
